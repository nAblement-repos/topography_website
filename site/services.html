Services

Maritime environmental compliance, mapped.

Everything we do supports one goal: helping our clients achieve and maintain compliance with global ballast water and biofouling management regulations.

Scoped per project · Hourly consultingScroll
01 / Core specialization

Ballast Water Management

IMO BWM Convention · USCG · EPA VGP

Support for compliance with ballast water requirements under the International Maritime Organization, the US Coast Guard, the US Environmental Protection Agency, national and regional authorities and ship classification societies.

  • IMO International Convention for the Control and Management of Ships’ Ballast Water and Sediments
  • US Coast Guard ballast water regulations
  • EPA Vessel General Permit (VGP)
  • National and regional requirements
  • Classification society requirements
  • Interpretation across trade routes and flag states

When clients call

When you need a clear reading of which requirements apply to your vessels and trade, and a realistic plan for meeting them.

02 / Core specialization

Biofouling Management

Hull & niche-area biosecurity

Biofouling on ship hulls is the other major way aquatic invasive species travel. We apply more than two decades of ballast water experience to the international biofouling management regulations, which we are helping to develop.

  • Guidance on emerging international biofouling management requirements
  • Biofouling management planning and program development
  • Connection to technology, service providers and the scientific community

When clients call

When you want to get ahead of biofouling requirements and build management practices that are practical, defensible and ready for what comes next.

03

Compliance Program Development

Plans · Contingency · Monitoring · Training

Environmental compliance programs for ships, built to work in the real conditions on board.

  • Ballast water management plans
  • Contingency planning
  • Compliance monitoring programs
  • Crew training programs
04

Program & Project Management

Scope · Coordinate · Deliver

Compliance programs and projects managed with honesty and integrity, so your goals are met as efficiently and cost-effectively as possible.

We coordinate among owners, technology suppliers, service providers and regulators so that each part of the work reaches the right party at the right time.

05

BWMS Approvals & Risk Assessment

Toxicology · Dossiers · Data

In-depth knowledge of chemical-based ballast water management systems, and of the chemistry, aquatic toxicology and human health questions that come with them.

  • Approval support through classification societies and international administrations
  • Aquatic toxicology research for chemical-based systems
  • Environmental & human health risk assessment of disinfection by-products
  • Dossier preparation and data analysis
  • Regulatory agency interactions
  • Onboard testing logistics
06

Regulatory Insight & Access

IMO · Global network

Direct access to a subject matter expert who takes part at the International Maritime Organization and follows global biosecurity regulation as it develops.

Clients also benefit from long-standing connections across the regulatory and scientific communities and from a global network of service providers.
